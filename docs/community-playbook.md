# Zone 2 Club community playbook

How to start the movement before (and alongside) the software.

## The promise

> Twice a week, ride or walk easy with interesting people and talk about something that matters. You'll get fitter without noticing.

## Session formats

| Format | Length | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Topic ride** | 45 min | "Founders ride: hiring your first engineer" |
| **Language walk** | 30 min | "Spanish B1 conversation walk" |
| **Book / podcast club** | 45–60 min | Discuss one chapter or episode listened to beforehand |
| **Base-building chat** | 60 min | Marathon, Ironman or HYROX athletes in base phase |
| **Open office hours** | 30 min | A host answers questions in their field |
| **Private 1:1** | 20–45 min | Mentor, coach, co-founder walk |

## Running a session (host checklist)

1. Post it in the channel at least 48 hours ahead: title, topic, time with time zone, activity, and voice-chat link.
2. Start the Telegram voice chat 5 minutes early. Greet each person by name.
3. Open by asking everyone for one line: name, what they're on (bike, treadmill or outside), and how they feel.
4. Around minute 10, do a talk-test check-in: "Can everyone still talk in full sentences? If not, ease off."
5. Keep it to one or two topics. Bring three prompt questions as backup.
6. Close: one takeaway each, then announce the next session.
7. After: post a recap and send the feedback form.

## Rituals that make it a movement

- **Fixed weekly slots** (for example, Tuesday 7:00 and Thursday 18:00 club rides). People return to habits, not one-off events.
- **Streaks:** consecutive weeks with at least one session.
- **Zone 2 minutes** as the shared unit, with monthly community totals ("The club rode 4,210 Zone 2 minutes in October").
- **Hosts are heroes:** feature hosts, give them tools first, and later a share of revenue from paid sessions.
- **Ambassadors** in endurance clubs, run clubs and Zwift communities.

## What to measure every week

- New attendees, and returning attendees in weeks 1 and 4
- Sessions held, and average attendance per session
- Self-reported time in zone
- Free-text "best moment" answers. These are the source of the marketing.

## Where to find the first 100 people

1. Zwift clubs and Discord ride groups (they already ride while talking)
2. Running and triathlon clubs in base-building season
3. Remote-work and founder Telegram communities
4. The founder's own network: personal invites, not posts
