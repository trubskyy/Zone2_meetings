# Market and competitors

*Last researched: 2026-10-12. Several sources are from 2021 or earlier. Re-check before external use.*

## Who already does part of this

| Player | What it is | Lesson for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Spot (Spot Meetings)** | A mobile-first, audio-only app for walking meetings, with AI notes and calendar integration. It raised about $1.9M (led by Chapter One) and then a $5M seed round (led by Kleiner Perkins) in 2021, and had a beta waitlist of about 5,000. We found no news after 2021. | This is the closest prior attempt. It was validated and funded, then apparently faded. **Asking people to switch meeting tools is the hard part.** Action: find out what happened before pitching investors. |
| **Zwift + Discord** | Group rides on Zwift run voice through Discord channels, which is a workaround. Riders have asked Zwift for built-in voice chat for years. | Indoor cyclists show real, badly served demand. The complaints are juggling two apps and audio dropouts. This is our best first audience. |
| **BONX** | An earpiece and app for hands-free group voice for up to about 10 cyclists. | Outdoor group audio already exists as hardware. We shouldn't compete on audio hardware. |
| **WalkSteady** | A Mac app that stabilises your webcam while you walk on a desk treadmill during Zoom, Meet or Teams calls. | People want to move *during their existing calls*. This supports the companion-not-replacement approach for teams. |
| **DeskWalker, Zone 2 Plus, Zone 2: Heart Rate Training** | Apps that track walking-pad or Zone 2 sessions on their own. | Tracking alone is a commodity. None of them has a social layer. |
| **Strava** | Over 1M clubs, new messaging and group chats, and club memberships up more than 59% in 2025. | The community layer is moving quickly. We integrate with Strava (post-session recaps) rather than compete with it. |
| **Peloton** | Has had a corporate wellness offering since 2021. | Company wellness budgets exist, and incumbents are already selling into them. |
| **Telegram voice chats / Discord** | Free general-purpose group voice. | Inside Telegram we use native voice chat. We don't rebuild audio. |

## Where the gap is

Nobody combines all three of these:
1. **Zone 2 as the shared goal.** It has a clear health story and a natural talk-test fit.
2. **Scheduled sessions on a topic** with hosts, that is, a community calendar.
3. **Live zone feedback** that respects privacy (zone rather than BPM).

## Who comes first

1. **Indoor cyclists and trainer owners** who already ride socially (Zwift and club riders). They're used to group rides and already own heart-rate straps.
2. **Endurance athletes in base-building phases** (marathon, Ironman, HYROX). They need many hours of Zone 2 and get bored doing it.
3. **Remote workers with a walking pad or bike**, for 1:1s and small private sessions.
4. **Coaches** who run client check-ins.

Corporate teams come later, once we have retention data.

## Why Telegram

Telegram reports about 1B monthly users and has strong community tools: channels, groups, voice chats, bots and Mini Apps. Its audience is strongest outside the US corporate world. That fits the community side well and the enterprise side poorly. Hence two surfaces from one codebase.
